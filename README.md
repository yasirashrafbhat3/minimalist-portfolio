# Minimalist Portfolio

Please simplify and redesign my existing portfolio website.

My previous design direction became too complicated and visually cluttered. I want a clean, organized, minimal but premium personal portfolio.

Design direction:

Dark charcoal or near-black background.

White and soft grey typography.

Use warm orange only as a subtle accent where it genuinely suits the design.

Clean modern fonts, large but controlled headings, balanced spacing.

My real photograph should be the main visual in the hero section, preferably as a large cinematic background or integrated portrait.

Add a subtle dark overlay to keep text readable.

Do not generate a fake face. Keep an image placeholder until I upload my photo.

Website structure:

Home: My name, a short introduction, my photograph, and buttons for View Projects and Contact Me.

About: My background, B.Tech AIML education at Atria Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, and interests in Data Engineering and AI/ML.

Projects: Four simple, organized project cards for Student Data Pipeline, CleanSlate, Corporate Expense Anomaly Auditor, and DataDrift. Show their actual status and available GitHub links.

Contact: GitHub, LinkedIn placeholder, and email placeholder.

Visual requirements:

Make everything easy to understand at first glance.

Use consistent alignment and spacing.

Use simple project cards.

Keep navigation minimal.

Use subtle hover effects and smooth scrolling only.

Make it look excellent on mobile and desktop.

Avoid excessive animations, 3D, neon colors, glassmorphism, unnecessary sections, decorative elements, and complicated layouts.

Do not invent information, achievements, experience, links, or statistics.

Most importantly, prioritize simplicity, clarity, personal identity, and premium presentation. Do not add features just to make the website look impressive.
Simplify my existing personal portfolio into a clean, premium, modern and fully editable website.

Design:

Dark charcoal background with white text and subtle orange accents only where they suit the design.

Use my real photograph as a cinematic hero background with a subtle dark overlay.

Beautiful typography, clean spacing and organized sections.

Add a little magic through smooth scrolling, subtle animations and elegant hover effects.

Sections:

Home

About

Projects

Skills

Contact

Important requirements:

Make all personal content, photos, skills, projects and links easy for me to update.

Create a simple, reusable project structure so I can add new projects easily.

Keep my project descriptions and statuses accurate.

Make every button and navigation link functional.

Ensure mobile and desktop responsiveness.

Keep the code clean and beginner-friendly.

Preserve my existing working information and links.

Do not overcomplicate the website. No excessive 3D, heavy animations, unnecessary sections, fake statistics or decorative clutter.

The final result should be simple to understand, easy to manage, slightly magical, and professionally designed. Do not rebuild unnecessarily or introduce complicated features.
